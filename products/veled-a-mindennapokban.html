Veled a mindennapokban

A thirty-day conversation with God.

A Catholic prayer journal that makes room for honest questions, not just perfect words. Written in Hungarian.

  • 30 days
  • 84 pages
  • A5

The printed manuscript is in Hungarian. English, German and French pages describe the product; they are not translated printed editions.

Who is it for?

For readers who want to bring their Catholic faith into everyday life, including times of joy, anger, uncertainty and silence.

What's inside?

Thirty days of Bible references (Káldi-Neovulgáta), brief guideposts to the Catechism of the Catholic Church, personal questions, writing pages, weekly reflections and space to bring a thought home from Mass.

How to use it

Read the day's passage, then choose one question. A sentence is enough. If a day feels too much, skip it: there is nothing to catch up on.

A place for the questions you carry

Faith and doubt, gratitude and disappointment, boundaries, responsibility, forgiveness, exhaustion and beginning again. You can bring all of it into prayer.

Thirty days of honest questions

God, can we talk — even without beautiful words?

A Catholic prayer journal that leaves room for joy, anger, doubt and gratitude instead of asking every prayer to sound confident.

01 · Does this sound familiar?

The everyday challenge

There are days when even prayer feels difficult because uncertainty, anger or tiredness leave you without the right words.

02 · Inside the publication

A useful starting point

Thirty daily topics, KNB and KEK references, and space for your own thoughts provide a personal framework for prayer without demanding perfect answers.

The 84-page A5 Hungarian journal presents thirty daily themes. Its questions include “Can I be angry with you?”, “Can I rest?” and “Forgiving — what next?”. They are invitations to think and pray, not tests of faith.

The text draws on the Káldi-Neovulgáta Bible (KNB) and the Catechism of the Catholic Church (KEK), with references and space for writing or looking back. One sentence is a worthwhile response; so is leaving a question open.

It is intended for personal reflection and prayer. No official ecclesiastical endorsement is claimed, and the journal does not replace pastoral care, psychological help or urgent assistance.
