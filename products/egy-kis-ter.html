Egy kis tér

A reflection workbook for adults

The printed source material shown is in Hungarian. Other-language descriptions are provided for orientation only.

Who it is for

For readers aged 18 and over who want to think through overload, decisions and relationship boundaries.

What is inside

24 everyday situations, personal stories, prompts, three worked examples and six reusable worksheets. This is a dated working manuscript, not a reviewed therapeutic method.

How to use it

Choose one situation that feels familiar and write just a few words. Skip anything that feels unsafe or overwhelming.
