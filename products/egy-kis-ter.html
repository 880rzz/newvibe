Egy kis tér

Reflective workbook for adults

The publications shown were written in Hungarian. The site descriptions in other languages do not imply translated printed editions.

Who is it for?

Adults aged 18+ reflecting on overload and relationships

What is inside?

24 everyday situations, personal stories, three fully worked examples and six separate worksheets

How to use it

Find a familiar situation. A few words are enough, and you can skip what feels too difficult.
