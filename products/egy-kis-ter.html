A little room for yourself

When everyone needs something from you, where do you fit in?

This adult workbook starts with days when helping others comes naturally, but the promises you make to yourself are the first to disappear.

Does this sound familiar?

When you keep saying yes to others, your own time and boundaries can quietly disappear.

What can this publication offer?

Twenty-four situations, three completed examples and six worksheets give you space to name what matters and consider one manageable step.

Twenty-four everyday situations explore overcommitting, postponing, guilt, personal boundaries and asking for help. Edited personal experiences are presented alongside clearly distinguished illustrative examples, not clinical case studies.

Three fully completed samples show what honest, imperfect notes can look like. Six separate worksheets offer room for your own experience. You do not need to move through the book in order or answer every question. A handful of words can be enough.

It is a reflection tool for adults, not therapy or diagnosis. Some situations suggest a small next step; others may help you recognise that support from another person is more important than filling out a page.
