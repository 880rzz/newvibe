The everyday challenge

02 · Inside the publication

A useful starting point

The same complete annual, monthly, weekly and meeting-note structure as the colourful edition comes with a restrained dark cover.

The yearly, monthly and weekly layouts give structure to teaching dates and the smaller jobs around them. There is space for meeting notes, expenses and looking back on the work of a busy period.

The darker, minimal cover does not mean reduced content. Both versions have matching interior pages. You are simply choosing the style that feels natural to use every day.

Write down the major deadlines first, then turn to the week. Give yourself room for imperfect notes — a few useful words may be more valuable than a flawless page.
