Who it is for

For teachers who want to keep classroom responsibilities and personal plans in one place.

Monthly and weekly planning, teaching notes, meeting summaries, spending, personal goals and reflections. This version has the illustrated warm-colour cover.

How to use it

Use the monthly overview to see what is coming, then return to the weekly page. Leave space for your own notes.
