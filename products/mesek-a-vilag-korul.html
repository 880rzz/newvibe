Who is it for?

For families, children reading with adults, and educators looking for stories to discuss together.

What's included?

Forty-two complete stories, with their countries of association and genres identified; illustrated opening pages and source references for the original versions. Longer tales continue across pages.

How to explore it

Pick a country or a story rather than reading in order. Take time to talk about what felt familiar, surprising or different.

02 · Inside the publication

A useful starting point

Forty-two stories linked to forty-two countries are gathered in a 131-page Hungarian reader, with information about their geographical and cultural connections.

The 131-page A4 reader travels through Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas and Oceania. It includes Befana's search from Italy, the Ukrainian mitten story and Ghana's Anansi, along with winter tales, animal stories and literary works.

Each selection identifies its country and the genre or tradition behind it. Colour opening pages, continuation sheets for longer texts and source references are part of the editorial structure. Forty-two companion colouring pages form a separate publication.

Choose an entire region or just one story for the afternoon. Some translation and commercial-use rights still require separate verification before fulfilment. An email preorder request is not an automatic delivery promise.
