Mesék a világ körül

Stories to read together, from one corner of the world to another.

An A4 Hungarian reading collection of folk tales, legends and literary stories associated with 42 countries. It includes winter and Christmas traditions alongside other kinds of tales.

  • 131 pages
  • A4
  • 42 countries · 42 stories

The book is currently written in Hungarian. This page is an English description, not an English print edition.

Who is it for?

For families, children reading with adults, and educators looking for stories to discuss together.

What's included?

Forty-two complete stories, with their countries of association and genres identified; illustrated opening pages and source references for the original versions. Longer tales continue across pages.

How to explore it

Pick a country or a story rather than reading in order. Take time to talk about what felt familiar, surprising or different.

Discover the companion

The companion coloring set has 42 separate black-and-white pages.
