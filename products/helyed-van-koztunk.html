What is inside?

When words are difficult

A card gives difficult conversations somewhere to begin

Talking about exclusion, bullying or asking for help can be hardest at the first sentence. These illustrated cards invite a child and a trusted adult to begin together.

The set consists of 36 illustrated, double-sided situation cards. One side opens a scene; questions on the reverse invite conversation. There is no score, correct answer or obligation to share something a child does not want to discuss.

The subjects include teasing, repeated exclusion, online abuse, personal boundaries and finding someone safe to tell. A separately produced sixteen-panel folding guide provides additional language for requesting help. It is a separate support item, not automatically included in the card set.

The cards encourage listening and connection; they cannot replace child protection, emergency help or appropriate professional support. In a dangerous situation, seeking safety and help from a trusted adult takes priority.
