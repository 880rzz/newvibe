Helyed van köztünk

Conversation cards about bullying and exclusion

The printed source material shown is in Hungarian. Other-language descriptions are provided for orientation only.

Who it is for

For children, their parents, teachers and other trusted adults.

What is inside

36 illustrated situations with a question or conversation prompt on the back; the associated 16-part fold-out covers seeking help and safety.

How to use it

Choose one card, read the short situation together and allow everyone to pass. If someone is unsafe, seek real help rather than using the cards as a substitute.
