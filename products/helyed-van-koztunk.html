Helyed van köztünk

Conversation cards about bullying and exclusion

The publications shown were written in Hungarian. The site descriptions in other languages do not imply translated printed editions.

Who is it for?

Children, parents, teachers and trusted adults

What is inside?

36 illustrated, double-sided situation cards with questions on the reverse. A separate 16-panel safety and help leaflet exists as a print source; its use is documented separately.

How to use it

Choose one card, talk together, and allow everyone to pass. If someone is in danger, seek actual professional help.
