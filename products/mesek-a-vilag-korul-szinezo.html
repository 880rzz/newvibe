Mesék a világ körül – 42 színező

The stories stay with you a little longer when you can color them.

Forty-two A4 black-and-white coloring pages associated with the 42 tales from the Hungarian reading collection Mesék a világ körül.

  • 42 pages
  • A4
  • 42 pages · companion coloring set

The book is currently written in Hungarian. This page is an English description, not an English print edition.

Who is it for?

For children who enjoy coloring after listening to or reading a story, at home or in guided creative activities.

What's inside?

One outlined illustration per story, with the Hungarian story title and associated country indicated on each page.

How to use it

Choose the matching story, then color the scene your own way. Colored pencils and a little unhurried time are enough.

Discover the companion

The companion reading collection includes 42 Hungarian stories and source references.
