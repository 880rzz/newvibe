Lépésről lépésre · 2027

2027 planner designed for adults with ADHD

The printed source material shown is in Hungarian. Other-language descriptions are provided for orientation only.

Who it is for

Adults who want a flexible structure for attention, everyday tasks and resuming after missed days.

What is inside

A 2027 calendar, weekly spreads, household routines, finances, errands, learning plans, monthly reviews, a daily focus sheet and a gentle-day sheet.

How to use it

You do not have to fill every field. Begin with the current month or the next small step; blank days do not have to be made up.
