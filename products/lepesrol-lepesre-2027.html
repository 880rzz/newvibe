Planning at your own pace

A planner you can return to after the blank days

The 2027 ADHD planner for adults is designed around the freedom to begin again instead of keeping a perfect record every day.

The 200-page A4 Hungarian edition moves from a year-at-a-glance view through monthly calendars to weekly spreads. Each week uses facing pages: meals and home tasks on the left, the weekly plan on the right. Monthly sections make space for bills, administration, shopping, learning and reflection.

The introductory prompts ask about strengths, attention, getting started and asking for practical help. You can choose up to three priorities for the year. A daily focus page and a gentler-day page appear towards the end of the book.

Start with the current month. You are not expected to complete the weeks you missed. This is a practical planning companion, not a treatment, diagnosis or measure of daily productivity.
