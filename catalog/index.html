A few useful answers

Can I buy these directly here?

Not yet. There are no prices or purchase links on this site until the separate webshop is available.

Are the printed products already published?

The card set is shown through genuine product photographs. The planners have print files, and the workbook is a manuscript. New product visuals are illustrative mock-ups.

Do these replace professional help?

No. They are prompts and planning aids, not treatments, diagnoses or professional safeguarding services.
