For Every Path

A person behind the pages. Not an invented company.

For Every Path is an independent creative project by Sajbán-Frőhner Alexandra. A separate company and active webshop do not yet exist. The planned domain is foreverypath.com; the current canonical website remains on GitHub Pages.

AI & Editorial Trust

Some editorial texts, illustrations and digital cover visualisations were made with AI assistance. According to the publisher, all final editions have completed professional approval. The Helyed van köztünk card set has genuine product photographs. These publications are not medical, diagnostic or therapeutic services.

The publications shown were written in Hungarian. The site descriptions in other languages do not imply translated printed editions.

Veled a mindennapokban is an 84-page Hungarian A5 Catholic prayer journal, featuring Bible and Catechism references and disclosed AI editorial assistance. The publisher confirms professional approval of the final edition; the illustrated cover is a digital visualisation, not a product photograph.

The Diófa Műhely collection includes the 131-page Mesék a világ körül (42 countries), a 42-page coloring companion, and the 139-page Na, most mit csináljak? (32 stories for ages 8–11). Their illustrated covers are digital previews, not actual product photographs. Stories inspired by Kóka do not imply endorsement by a school, parish or local institution.

Email pre-order requests are accepted for all nine editions at diofamuhelykozosseg@gmail.com. A request does not confirm a purchase or reserve stock. Prices, availability, payment and delivery terms are agreed before confirmation. No payment is collected by this site.
