For Every Path

A person behind the pages. Not an invented company.

For Every Path is an independent creative project by Sajbán-Frőhner Alexandra. A separate company and active webshop do not yet exist. The planned domain is foreverypath.com; the current canonical website remains on GitHub Pages.

AI and editorial transparency

Some editorial texts, illustrations and digital cover visualisations were made with AI assistance. According to the publisher, all final editions have completed professional approval. The Helyed van köztünk card set has genuine product photographs. These publications are not medical, diagnostic or therapeutic services.

The publications shown were written in Hungarian. The site descriptions in other languages do not imply translated printed editions.

Veled a mindennapokban is an 84-page Hungarian A5 Catholic prayer journal. Its editorial content has the publisher's professional approval. The original source identifies AI assistance and biblical and Catechism references. No official institutional endorsement by the Catholic Church is claimed. The displayed image is an illustrative concept, not a product photograph.

Three further Hungarian A4 publications appear under Diófa Műhely: Mesék a világ körül (131 pages, 42 countries), its 42 coloring pages, and Na, most mit csináljak? (139 pages, 32 stories for ages 8–11). Their content is professionally approved according to the publisher. The covers are digital mock-ups; institutional partnerships and separate commercial licences are not asserted by this page. The Kóka-inspired settings are fictional.

Email pre-order requests are accepted for all nine editions at diofamuhelykozosseg@gmail.com. A message does not confirm a purchase or reserve stock. Prices, payment, lawful publication permissions and delivery terms are agreed individually before confirmation. No online payment is collected.
