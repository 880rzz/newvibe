For Every Path · About the project

A person behind the pages, not a company that does not exist.

For Every Path is a developing project curated and compiled by Sajbán-Frőhner Alexandra. There is currently no separate legal company presented as the operator, and no operating webshop. The domain foreverypath.com is planned but not yet configured as the live website.

How these pages are made

The original publications are Hungarian. The 2027 planners are based on print-ready layout files. The card set has genuine product photography. The adult reflection workbook is an AI-assisted, extended editorial draft dated 19 November 2026; independent professional review and user testing are still outstanding. Claims of clinical effectiveness are not made.

AI-assisted product visualisations show possible physical formats, not manufacturing samples. Genuine photos are identified separately. Before checkout is enabled, the provider’s verified legal details, customer terms, returns and privacy information must be published.
