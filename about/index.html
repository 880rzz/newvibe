For Every Path

A person behind the pages. Not an invented company.

For Every Path is an independent creative project by Sajbán-Frőhner Alexandra. A separate company and active webshop do not yet exist. The planned domain is foreverypath.com; the current canonical website remains on GitHub Pages.

All nine products can now be requested for pre-order by emailing diofamuhelykozosseg@gmail.com. This opens your own email client; it is not an online checkout. Alexandra must reply before an order can be considered accepted. Please send only the product and quantity, not payment information. The Mesék a világ körül publications additionally depend on unresolved copyright and translation clearance.

AI & Editorial Trust

Some editorial texts, illustrations and concept visuals were made with AI assistance. The workbook Egy kis tér is a manuscript dated 19 November 2026, with independent professional review and reader testing still outstanding. The existing Helyed van köztünk card pack has real photographs. None of the products is a medical, diagnostic or therapeutic service.

The publications shown were written in Hungarian. The site descriptions in other languages do not imply translated printed editions.

The new Catholic prayer journal, Veled a mindennapokban, is an 84-page Hungarian A5 manuscript compiled on 18 November 2026 with declared AI assistance. Biblical and Catechism citations are identified in the source. Independent theological review and ecclesiastical approval have not been verified; its image is a concept visualisation, not an actual printed copy.

Three further Hungarian A4 manuscripts are presented as Diófa Műhely publications: Mesék a világ körül (131 pages, 42 countries), 42 matching coloring pages, and Na, most mit csináljak? (139 pages, 32 stories for ages 8–11). All new cover visuals are digitally constructed mock-ups rather than product photographs. The 42-story source itself flags unresolved translation and copyright questions, so commercial rights clearance is not claimed. Fictional Kóka-inspired stories do not imply endorsement by any school, parish or local institution.
