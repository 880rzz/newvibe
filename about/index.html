About Alexandra

Take your time. Every path starts somewhere.

Diófa Műhely · Sajbán-Frőhner Alexandra

I love helping. I really do.

Alexandra enjoys working with children, organising things and finding a way to help when someone needs it. She also knows what it feels like to give everyone a part of your day and find very little time left for yourself. That human experience is part of what shapes Diófa Műhely.

Not ready-made answers. A question worth asking.

In the personal introduction to Egy kis tér, Sajbán-Frőhner Alexandra writes about the joy of helping, the yes that comes too quickly and the effort of setting boundaries. She does not claim to have a solution for every situation. Her publications also hold her own questions, stories and observations.

The conversation cards, journals, planners, stories and activity books invite us to pause, find words, make a plan or create something together. Everything does not have to be solved today.

How the publications are made

The concepts, personal stories and editorial direction are Alexandra's. Some edited texts, illustrations and digital cover previews were created with AI assistance. The creator reports that the final editions have received professional editorial approval. That is not a clinical, therapeutic, official ecclesiastical or institutional endorsement.

The Helyed van köztünk cards have real product photographs; several other covers are digital mockups. The printed publications are in Hungarian. Translated web descriptions are not translated print editions.
